Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 29, 2025 • Effective immediately

1 Introduction

Welcome to Railways Science PYQs ("we", "our", "us"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and personal data. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application.

We do NOT sell, rent, or share your personal data with any third parties for marketing purposes.

2 Information We Collect

We collect minimal information necessary to provide our services:

  • Phone Number: Used for user identification and premium purchase verification.
  • Name (Optional): Used for personalizing your app experience.
  • Payment Information: Processed securely via Razorpay. We do NOT store your card details, UPI ID, or bank information.
  • App Usage Data: Anonymous usage statistics such as quiz scores, streaks, and progress to improve your learning experience.

3 How We Use Your Information

  • To create and manage your user profile within the app.
  • To verify and activate your premium subscription.
  • To track your learning progress (quiz scores, streaks, bookmarks).
  • To improve our app and provide a better study experience.

4 Data Storage & Security

Your profile data (phone number, name) is stored securely in Google Sheets with restricted access. All data transmission is encrypted using industry-standard HTTPS/SSL protocols.

  • Payment processing is handled entirely by Razorpay, a PCI-DSS compliant payment gateway.
  • Your app preferences and progress are stored locally on your device using encrypted local storage.
  • We do not store any financial or banking information on our servers.

5 Third-Party Services

Our app uses the following third-party services:

  • Razorpay: For secure payment processing. Razorpay Privacy Policy
  • Google Sheets API: For user profile management and premium status tracking.
  • jsDelivr CDN: For delivering question content efficiently.

6 Children's Privacy

Our app is an educational application suitable for students of all ages preparing for railway competitive exams. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13 without parental consent.

7 Your Rights

  • Access: You can view your stored profile data within the app's Profile section.
  • Deletion: You can request complete deletion of your data by contacting us at the email below.
  • Correction: You can update your name and other profile details directly from the app.
